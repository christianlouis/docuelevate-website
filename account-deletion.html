Account deletion

Delete from the application

Self-service account deletion is not currently available in the hosted DocuElevate service. Use the email request below. Do not rely on an account-deletion link until the hosted service announces that the feature is enabled.

On a self-hosted version with account deletion enabled, open Profile → Delete account in the web or iOS application, review the consequences and type the confirmation phrase. Deletion starts only after you confirm. If your installation does not offer deletion, contact its operator.

When enabled, the application removes account-owned documents, processing data, settings, sharing links and saved connections. A failed cleanup remains blocked for normal use and can be retried. A legal hold can prevent deletion until the operator resolves it.

Support and retained copies

To request removal of a DocuElevate account and associated data, email docuelevate@christian-louis.de from the account address and write “Account deletion” in the subject.

We will confirm the request and identify any data that must be retained for legal reasons. The request covers the DocuElevate account, stored document metadata, processing records and connected provider credentials under the hosted service. Files already exported to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or another destination must be deleted there by the account owner unless that provider offers a separate deletion action.

Independent exported copies remain with their destination providers. Backups expire according to the operator’s retention policy. A minimal account-deletion marker prevents queued jobs and old sessions from recreating erased data; it is not an active user profile.
