Account deletion

To request removal of a DocuElevate account and associated data, email docuelevate@christian-louis.de from the account address and write “Account deletion” in the subject.

We will confirm the request and identify any data that must be retained for legal reasons. The request covers the DocuElevate account, stored document metadata, processing records and connected provider credentials under the hosted service. Files already exported to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or another destination must be deleted there by the account owner unless that provider offers a separate deletion action.

Account deletion is currently requested by email. Self-service deletion is not yet available.
